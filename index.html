Delete Your Account & Data

You are always in control of your data. Here is how to permanently delete your Notifiqo account and everything associated with it.

1

In-app (fastest)

Open Notifiqo → Settings → sign in with the same Google account → tap “Delete Account” under your profile → confirm. Cloud data, local data and your account are deleted immediately.

2

By email request

No longer have the app? Email support.notifiqo@gmail.com from the Google account you used, with the subject “Delete My Notifiqo Account”. We process requests within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • All notifications, automation rules and shipment records synced to the cloud
  • All notification history and settings stored on your device
  • Your Google-linked Notifiqo account itself
  • Encrypted backups in your own Google Drive stay there — delete those files from Drive yourself
