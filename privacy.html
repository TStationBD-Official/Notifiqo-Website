Privacy Policy

How we protect your data

Data Collection

Notifiqo captures notification content (title, body, sender, application name and icon) through the Android notification access you grant. It is stored on your device, and one-time codes (OTPs) are detected on the device itself.

Media & Attachments

Notification images and file attachments are kept in the app’s private storage on your device. If Cloud Sync is on, photo previews are also uploaded to a private Telegram channel used by Notifiqo, and they are encrypted first when Encrypted Sync is on.

Cloud Synchronization

If you turn on Cloud Sync and sign in with Google, your notifications, rules and shipments are synced to Firebase Cloud Firestore so you can use them on other devices. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.

Sharing Features

When you explicitly share your notifications with another user via the “Looking Up” feature, that user will be able to view your synced notification history in real-time. You maintain full control and can revoke access at any time.

Data Security

Optional Encrypted Sync encrypts your notifications with your own password before upload; without it, synced notifications are stored in Firestore unencrypted. Local data is protected by Android’s app isolation, and deleting data asks for your PIN when one is set. To let you regain access, your encryption key is also stored in Firestore wrapped by your password and by your recovery key; without one of them it cannot be read, and we cannot recover it for you.

User Rights

“Reset all data” erases history, settings and unlocked features on this device and signs you out; it does not remove data already synced to the cloud. “Delete account” permanently erases your cloud notifications, rules and shipments, your local data and your account.

Third-Party Services

Notifiqo uses Google sign-in and Firebase, optional backups to your own Google Drive (only files the app creates), Google Play for purchases, and Unity Ads to show ads, which may use your advertising ID. The About screen loads company and app information from our public API.

App Catalog

To show the right icon and category for every app, Notifiqo keeps a shared catalog of apps. When Cloud Sync is on and “Share new apps” is on in Settings, the first time your phone sees an app that is not in the catalog, it adds that app’s package name, name, category and small icon (stored in the same private Telegram channel as photo previews) to the catalog. No notification content or personal data is included, and you can turn this off at any time.
